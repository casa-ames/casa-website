# CASA logo proposals

These are review assets, not yet integrated into the public CASA website.

## Colors

- CASA red: `#FF0000`
- True black: `#000000`
- Light gray: `#AAAAAA`
- Reverse white: `#FFFFFF`

## Construction

- The original CASA brush mark was traced from `public/images/casa-logo-source.jpg` into SVG paths.
- The organization name is converted to vector outlines rather than live text.
- Files ending in `transparent` have no background rectangle.
- Files ending in `on-white` include a white background deliberately.
- White variants are intended only for dark backgrounds.
- The square standalone mark remains the recommended favicon because the full name cannot remain legible at favicon sizes.
- The `wide` proposal places the complete legal name on one condensed line beneath the brush mark for a wider alternative to the two-line stacked lockup.

## Wide-lockup placement guide

- `wide-red-black`: primary choice on white and very light backgrounds.
- `wide-red-white`: reverse treatment retaining the CASA red mark on black or dark gray.
- `wide-white`: monochrome reverse treatment on black, dark gray, or sufficiently dark photography.

Use the transparent files for normal website placement. The explicit `on-white` and `on-black` files are included for exports or systems that do not preserve transparency reliably.
